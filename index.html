Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price range? ▼

Abayas range from SAR 150 to SAR 250 depending on style, fabric, and customization. Premium materials and bespoke designs may be higher.

How long is delivery? ▼

Standard delivery within Saudi Arabia is 7-10 working days. International orders depend on location. Confirm timeline with Sinu via WhatsApp.

Can I customize the size? ▼

Yes! All Abayas are custom-fitted. You can select standard sizes or provide your own measurements for a perfect fit.

What fabrics are available? ▼

We offer Nada Fabric, Crepe, Premium Silk, Wool Blend, and more. Each fabric is premium quality and carefully selected for comfort and elegance.
